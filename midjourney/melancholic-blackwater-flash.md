## Prompt

```text
Use the face from my reference photo (face reference), preserve the likeness and recognizability 100% — do not change the face shape, nose shape, eyebrow shape, lip shape, eye shape and color, or the proportions of the face and body.

Nighttime pool. The water appears dark, almost black or deep blue. The atmosphere of an enclosed nighttime party, slightly melancholic and infused with the aesthetic of hedonism.

Light and camera: Film photography (35mm film). Harsh direct on-camera flash aimed straight at the face. This technique removes excess shadows from the face and makes the skin glossy, but creates a sharp, dense black shadow behind the model (on the water).

Clothing: Ultra-minimalist swimsuit. Black one-piece, thin straps, fabric fitting tightly against the body.

Hairstyle: Wet hair look. The hair is carelessly slicked back, with several damp strands stuck to the face and neck, emphasizing realism.

Makeup (Eyes and Lips):
Eyes: The main accent is long, heavily coated, “spider” lashes (spider lashes / clumpy mascara in the style of Twiggy). Wet, glossy eyelids without bright eyeshadow.
Lips: Juicy and slightly swollen. A glossy tint effect in a dusty rose shade.
Skin: Wet and glowing from the water and flash, with visible skin texture, pores, and water droplets (no plastic retouching).

Manicure: Short square shape. Deep wine-colored polish.

Pose and camera angle: Medium shot (from the chest up while in the water). The woman stands relaxed in the water. In one hand, she holds a glass of chilled white wine (condensation droplets visible on the glass). Camera angle — slightly from above (eye-level or slight high angle), creating the feeling of reportage photography.

Facial expression: Relaxed, slightly arrogant, cold, with a piercing and confident gaze directly into the camera lens. Lips slightly parted.

Color grading and processing: Heavy film grain (ISO 3200), vignetting, and light film scratches. Cool tones of the night and water contrast with the bright flash and the pale golden color of the wine. High contrast. Photorealism.

Image quality like an iPhone 17 — slight digital noise, soft sharpness, slight grain, and the effect of a live, candid shot. High detail in the texture of the skin, pores, micro-hairs, fabric, hair, and props.
```
