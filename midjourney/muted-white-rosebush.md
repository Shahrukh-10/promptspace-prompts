## Prompt

```text
Create a portrait of the person in the uploaded reference image. Preserve identity 100% — preserve facial features, skin tone, eye color, hair color, hair texture, and recognizable body proportions exactly. Do not alter the face.

Place the person standing in a lush outdoor flower garden densely filled with clusters of white roses and abundant dark green foliage, beneath a clear pale-blue sky during soft late-afternoon daylight. The background should be filled with layers of flowering bushes extending naturally behind the subject, with distant greenery softly blurred to create depth. Compose the image vertically as a three-quarter to nearly full-body fashion portrait, photographed from approximately eye level at a moderate distance, with the person centered in the frame and surrounded by flowers. The person stands facing the camera with a relaxed upright posture, shoulders slightly angled, one arm positioned naturally behind the body while the other hand rests gently across the opposite forearm near the upper thigh. Keep the expression calm, confident, and subtly inviting, with the gaze directed toward the camera. Dress the person in a fitted cream or warm ivory long-sleeve mini dress with a deep rounded scoop neckline, close-fitting silhouette, softly ruched fabric around the waist and hips, and slightly flared or extended cuffs at the wrists. The lightweight stretchy fabric should show realistic gathering and natural folds around the body. Keep accessories minimal and understated. Style the hair long, straight, smooth, and naturally falling over the shoulders and down the front, with a subtle center or slightly off-center part; retain the user's original hair color exactly. Use soft natural sunlight from the front and slightly above, creating gentle highlights on the hair, shoulders, face, and cream fabric while maintaining soft shadows. The overall lighting should feel warm, flattering, and natural rather than studio-lit. Use a palette of creamy ivory, fresh deep green, white blossoms, and soft sky blue, creating a romantic, serene summer-garden atmosphere. Preserve individual flower petals, leaves, realistic fabric texture and folds, natural skin detail, fine hair strands, and authentic outdoor depth. Use an elegant contemporary lifestyle-fashion photography aesthetic with shallow depth of field, softly blurred floral background, realistic dynamic range, subtle warm color grading, gentle contrast, natural photographic grain, and believable camera rendering.

Keep the pose, wardrobe, setting, camera angle, and lighting exactly as described. Preserve natural anatomy, believable skin texture, and photographic realism. The result must look like a real photograph of the reference person taken in this scene — not a paste-in composite. Match the color grading and mood consistently across the whole frame.
```
