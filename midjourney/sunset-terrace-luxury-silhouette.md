## Prompt

```text
Photorealistic premium-level fashion photography, shot on an open terrace by the sea at sunset. Vertical full-body shot, the model is centered in the composition, the distance to the camera is about 3–4 meters. The shooting angle is from waist level or slightly lower, creating an effect of long legs and emphasizing the silhouette. The body is turned at a half-turn toward the camera, weight shifted to one leg, hips slightly offset to the side, forming an expressive S-curve figure. One hand is extended to the side and lightly resting on a glass terrace railing, the other is relaxed along the hip next to a small black bag on a thin strap under the arm. The head is slightly tilted forward and a bit to the side, the gaze is directed downward, creating a natural feeling of a candidly captured moment. The model wears a long slim-fitting floor-length dress with thin straps and soft draping around the figure, widening toward the bottom. Black pointed-toe heeled sandals, thin bracelets, compact black bag. In the background, the calm sea, dark silhouettes of mountains on the horizon, and a rich sunset sky with a smooth transition of shades from warm orange and peach to deep bluish-gray. On the right in the frame, large decorative grasses and coastal plants in a massive planter, adding depth and volume to the composition. The main light source is direct flash from a smartphone or camera, which brightly illuminates the figure in the foreground and creates contrast with the darker sunset background. Sharp fabric details, slight highlights on accessories and the glass railing, soft natural shadows without harsh black clipping. Atmosphere of a luxurious summer vacation at a Mediterranean resort, luxury lifestyle aesthetic, Instagram high-fashion editorial. Extremely realistic photography with no signs of AI generation, natural color reproduction, high detail, professional processing with preservation of material textures, 35–50mm equivalent lens, moderate depth of field with readability of the seascape and sunset sky in the background. Use the appearance of the person from the uploaded selfie, fully preserving their individual facial features, while accurately reproducing the pose, composition, clothing, lighting, angle, environment, and atmosphere of this frame. Photo taken on iPhone 17 Pro Max with flash.
```
