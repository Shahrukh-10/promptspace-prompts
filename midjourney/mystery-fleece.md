## Prompt

```text
A photorealistic outdoor portrait of a young man standing in a misty forest clearing during the early morning golden hour. He wears a thick earth-toned fleece zip-up jacket with the collar raised slightly, giving a rugged yet approachable outdoorsman aesthetic. His dark hair is tousled naturally, and he gazes off-camera with a contemplative, slightly mysterious expression. The background features dense evergreen trees fading into soft fog, with dappled sunlight filtering through the canopy creating volumetric light rays and warm highlights on his shoulders and the fleece texture. Shot with a full-frame camera, 105mm f/2.0 telephoto portrait lens, producing beautiful subject isolation with a dreamy, creamy bokeh that dissolves the forest into painterly green and gold tones. Natural daylight serves as the primary light source from camera-left, supplemented by ambient fog diffusion that softens shadows and creates an ethereal atmosphere. Color grading leans into desaturated greens and warm amber highlights with lifted shadows for a cinematic, editorial look. Fine fabric texture of the fleece is captured with exceptional detail. The composition is a medium close-up from chest level, subject placed slightly right of center, conveying solitude, quiet strength, and connection with nature.
```
