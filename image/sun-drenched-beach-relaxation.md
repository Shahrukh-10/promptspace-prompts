## Prompt

```text
Photorealistic summer top-down shot, captured almost vertically from a close distance on the beach: a tanned athletic man is lying on his back on a light-colored, slightly rumpled towel spread over dry sand. The frame shows the upper body shirtless, chest and shoulders illuminated by harsh direct sun, skin is a warm bronze with a natural sheen. The man is lying in a relaxed pose, one arm bent behind his head, the other raised to his forehead, partially shielding his eyes from the sun; thin bracelets on his wrist. He is wearing a dark green baseball cap with white vintage lettering “NORTH SHORE SURF CLUB”, the visor casts a shadow over the upper part of his face. He has short hair; short dark beard and mustache, calm facial expression, eyes in shadow. The composition is tight and natural, the body is shifted to the lower right part of the frame, the sandy surface occupies the top and left part of the image. The background is uneven beach sand with small stones, depressions, indentations, rough grainy texture, and warm beige-brown shades. The towel is white, with soft folds and dusty specks of sand along the edges. Lighting is midday, contrasting, with sharp shadows from the hand, cap, and relief of the sand; palette is sun-bleached, muted, warm sand, bronze, and dark green tones. Style is realistic beach lifestyle photography with a slightly vintage film treatment, soft grain, muted contrast, natural skin and sand textures, no staged glossiness. Camera feels like a mobile or compact camera, vertical format, 9:16 aspect ratio, slightly underexposed shadows under the cap, high detail of materials and an atmosphere of a hot, calm day by the sea.
```
