## Prompt

```text
Use the reference woman while preserving her exact facial identity, facial proportions and natural body proportions. Do not describe or intentionally alter her hair. Create an extremely realistic vertical smartphone photograph of an adult woman standing alone in a large field of tall dry grass during soft twilight just after sunset. She is positioned slightly to the right of center rather than perfectly centered, framed from approximately mid-thigh upward with an enormous cloudy pastel sky occupying most of the upper image. Her body faces the camera at a slight three-quarter angle, both hands placed casually behind her lower back, shoulders relaxed, chin gently raised and gaze directed slightly past the camera with a soft distant expression. Outfit: fitted ivory-white corset-style top with wide shoulder straps, square neckline, visible structured vertical boning and delicate textured fabric, paired with a lightweight layered semi-sheer white skirt beginning at the hips and falling loosely around the thighs, with soft lace and gauze layers creating irregular natural edges, plus a large silver cross pendant necklace. No elaborate accessories. Makeup: soft natural evening makeup, lightly bronzed complexion, subtle blush, defined brows, separated lashes and nude-pink lips, realistic skin texture. Background: endless brown-gold meadow, flat distant tree line and huge muted pink-grey clouds with pale blue openings. Shot casually by a friend on an iPhone 17 Pro Max rear camera, 1x lens, available twilight only, no flash, slight phone grain in the darker grass, mild softness, naturally low contrast and imperfect automatic white balance. NO professional bridal photography, NO cinematic sunset effects, NO artificial haze, NO dreamy glow, NO fashion campaign, NO skin smoothing, NO special effects. It should look like a beautiful but completely real phone photo taken during an evening walk through a field.
```
