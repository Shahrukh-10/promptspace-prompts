## Prompt

```text
Do not change the appearance (preserve identity). Create a realistic image. Young athletic man with a short neat haircut, pronounced muscle definition, broad shoulders, and a toned physique. Clean-shaven face, expressive jawline, natural skin tone, confident direct look into the camera, calm and serious facial expression. Location: professional photo studio with a minimalist light gray seamless backdrop, a simple modern chair with a gray seat and chrome metal frame, clean space without extra props. Framing: medium shot, framing from the knees up, camera positioned at eye level, the man is strictly in the center of the composition. Pose: the man is sitting backwards straddling the chair, his left forearm rests relaxed on the back of the chair, torso is slightly leaning forward, shoulders are open, looking directly into the lens, the pose is confident, calm, and masculine. Clothing: bare torso, loose dark gray sweatpants with elastic cuffs, dark high-top sneakers with light laces and a light sole, socks with a neat Dior logo. Soft even studio lighting with a diffused light source delicately highlights muscle definition and skin texture without harsh shadows. The color palette is monochrome, predominantly featuring light gray, graphite, black, and cream shades. Modern minimalist fashion photography with high detailing of skin, fabric, shoes, and the metal surface of the chair, clean editorial aesthetics, calm and expressive mood. Soft color grading, image quality like iPhone 17, pronounced grain, muted colors. Detailed rendering of skin, fabric, and props. Shot on a phone camera. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```
