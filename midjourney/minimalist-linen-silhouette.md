## Prompt

```text
A man stands in front of a large floor mirror, taking a mirror selfie with his smartphone, holding it at face level. One hand is in his trouser pocket, his body is slightly turned relative to the mirror, and his weight is shifted to one leg. He is looking at the phone screen. The shot is taken full-length, at eye level, and the composition is centered within the arched shape of the mirror. The main focus of the shot is the man's silhouette, clothing, and relaxed pose.

The man is wearing an oversized chocolate brown shirt (#6A4634) made of fine linen with a matte textured surface. The top buttons are undone, and the sleeves are slightly rolled up to the forearms. His loose, straight-leg trousers are made of soft white cotton fabric (#F5F4EF) with a matte surface and natural folds. He is wearing open-toe leather sandals in dark brown (#5A4333) with a smooth matte surface. He is wearing a metal watch bracelet with a satin sheen. The woman holds a light-colored iPhone with a smooth, glossy surface.

The scene takes place in a modern, minimalist apartment interior. A large arched mirror with a thin black frame takes up most of the frame. In the background are a gray sofa, a small round coffee table with a black metal frame, a light-colored rug with subtle stripes, wall-mounted graphic posters in black frames, and a geometric pendant lamp. The walls are painted a warm white (#F4F2EE), and the floor is light wood with a matte finish.

The lighting is natural, soft, and diffused. The main light source comes from a window off-frame, to the side of the mirror. The light evenly illuminates the interior and the man's figure, creating smooth transitions between light and shadow. The shadows are soft, shallow, and retain detail. The contrast is moderate. The light highlights the texture of the linen shirt, the fabric of the trousers, and the surface of the furniture without creating pronounced highlights.

Shot with a 50mm lens. Moderate depth of field. The focus is on the man's figure and his clothing. The interior remains clearly legible, but slightly softer in detail. Highly detailed fabrics, furnishings, leather, and material surfaces. Contemporary lifestyle interior photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.
```
