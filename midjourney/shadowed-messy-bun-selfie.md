## Prompt

```text
Preserve facial identity 100%. Selfie in the style of a grainy smartphone photo. Selfie from the front-facing camera. Extreme close-up of head and upper body. Shoulders relaxed and slightly tilted forward, right shoulder raised toward the ear, head tilted slightly toward the shoulder. Camera angle from below, frame slightly offset. Background is a white ceiling. Dim evening lighting, shadows from window blinds on the face, no flash. High messy bun with many stray strands near the face, one strand falling across the face. Light makeup, long eyelashes, eyeliner, lips with gloss. Black ribbed t-shirt with exposed seams print. Black and white processing, dimmed exposure. Dim lighting, bright saturation, light grain/noise for film aesthetics, low contrast, aesthetic, and shadows that emphasize facial features.
```
