## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded reference photo as the identity reference.

Preserve the person's facial identity exactly. Maintain the same face shape, facial proportions, hairstyle, hair color, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone, age, body proportions, and overall appearance. Do not beautify, stylize, or modify the person's identity.

Create an ultra-realistic black-and-white luxury fashion editorial portrait.

The subject is photographed in a timeless monochrome aesthetic with a calm, confident, and powerful presence. The portrait should feel like a campaign for a luxury fashion house, balancing minimalism with cinematic elegance.

Pose:

• Natural, relaxed posture.
• Direct eye contact or slightly off-camera gaze.
• Calm, confident facial expression.
• A thin wooden stick elegantly resting between the lips.
• Effortless editorial attitude.

Keep the hairstyle exactly as shown in the reference image.

Keep the clothing exactly as shown in the reference image whenever possible.

If a wardrobe change is explicitly requested, dress the subject in premium luxury fashion, such as:

• Tailored dark overcoat.
• Black turtleneck.
• Premium monochrome layering.
• Minimalist silver jewelry.
• Thick silver chain or elegant accessories.

The styling should remain refined, modern, and timeless.

Environment:

• Minimal architectural studio.
• Clean textured concrete or smooth matte wall.
• Minimal distractions.
• Luxury editorial atmosphere.

Lighting:

• Soft diffused daylight.
• Strong monochrome contrast.
• Deep blacks.
• Rich grayscale tonal separation.
• Soft matte highlights.
• Gentle shadow transitions.
• Sculptural facial lighting emphasizing cheekbones and jawline.

Camera:

• Hasselblad X2D medium format camera.
• 35mm lens.
• f/2.8 aperture.
• Natural shallow depth of field.
• Ultra-sharp focus on the eyes.
• Medium-format optical rendering.

Composition:

• Vertical 4:5.
• Tight editorial portrait.
• Clean luxury framing.
• Balanced negative space.

Skin:

• Ultra-realistic skin texture.
• Visible pores.
• Fine facial hairs.
• Natural complexion.
• Authentic skin translucency.
• Matte skin finish.
• No beauty filters.
• No plastic skin.
• Preserve any natural facial details such as freckles, scars or tattoos if they exist in the reference image.

Color grading:

• Rich monochrome.
• Deep blacks.
• Bright whites.
• Fine grayscale transitions.
• Subtle Kodak Tri-X inspired film grain.

Style references:

Saint Laurent.
Tom Ford.
Loewe.
Rick Owens.
Alexander McQueen.
Vogue.
Harper's Bazaar.
Interview Magazine.
Luxury fashion campaign.
Fine-art editorial portrait.

Photorealistic.
Ultra-detailed.
HDR.
8K.

Negative prompt:

Do not change the person's identity.
Do not alter facial features.
Do not change the hairstyle.
Do not add tattoos unless they already exist in the reference image.
Do not replace the clothing unless explicitly requested.
No cartoon.
No anime.
No CGI.
No AI beauty.
No glamour retouching.
No distorted anatomy.
No extra limbs.
No duplicated body parts.
No blurry face.
No low quality.
No watermark.
No logos.
No text.
```
