## Prompt

```text
Image 1 = SUBJECT. Recreate this scene with the same composition and mood, but replace the car with a Mercedes-Benz G 63 AMG.

MANDATORY IDENTITY (HIGHEST PRIORITY - DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Preserve exact facial features and identity from Image 1
- Keep real body proportions and height perception unchanged
- No face swap, no identity drift

MANDATORY CAR REPLACEMENT (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Replace original car with a black Mercedes-Benz G 63 AMG
- Car must be real-size accurate and physically proportional
- No oversized or exaggerated vehicle scaling

MANDATORY HUMAN-TO-CAR SCALE LOCK (CRITICAL - DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Subject must appear visually dominant in frame, car as secondary anchor
- Subject height in frame must be approximately 65-75% of visible car body height in same depth plane
- Car width must NOT exceed 1.35x subject full-body visual height in composition
- Keep realistic distance/perspective so car does not look giant
- If scale conflict occurs, prioritize correct human scale over car prominence

MANDATORY COMPOSITION (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Elevated top-down angle from above
- Vertical framing
- Subject standing/leaning near front area of car
- Similar spacing as reference
- Patterned stone pavement visible

MANDATORY STYLE (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Black-and-white (monochrome)
- Luxury editorial mood
- Crisp detail, balanced contrast

MANDATORY SUBJECT LOOK (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Relaxed confident posture
- Sunglasses
- Light linen-style shirt, light shorts, loafers
- Minimal accessories

MANDATORY LIGHTING (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Natural daylight converted to monochrome
- Soft shadows, clean tonal separation
- Preserve texture in skin, clothing, pavement, and car paint

ANTI-DRIFT ENFORCEMENT (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- Keep camera angle fixed
- Keep subject-to-car scale fixed (no giant car effect)
- Keep only car model changed to G 63 AMG
- If conflict occurs, identity + scale + composition locks override all style choices

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (DO NOT CHANGE - LOCKED):
- No oversized SUV perspective
- No fisheye stretching on car
- No camera-distance change that shrinks subject
- No non-G63 vehicle
- No text
- No watermark
- No logo
```
