## Prompt

```text
Do not change facial features.
Shot on iPhone.
Realistic photograph of a young woman with warm tanned skin and very long hair, lying on her back on one side on fine light brown sand on a beach, taken from a high angle overhead selfie, her head slightly tilted to the side, and her face turned toward the lens with a relaxed expression, eyes looking straight up with laminated eyelashes and precise black winged eyeliner, pinkish-nude lips, Three small delicate light pink and white tropical flowers with pink centers rest in her loose flowing hair on one side. The hair is beautifully scattered on the sand. while her bare neck, collarbones, and upper chest reveal smooth slightly tanned skin with subtle shadows.
Strictly Horizontal format 9:16
Do not blur the background
Lowered exposure.
Hyperrealism
No sun.
```
