## Prompt

```text
Create a portrait, preserve facial identity, facial features, and hair color 100%. Close-up lifestyle shot of a young woman in a bright swimming pool, shot from above at a diagonal angle, almost like a spontaneous phone photograph by the water. The woman is located in the lower right part of the composition, leaning against the light-beige wet edge of the pool, on which stands a Mojito: a tall, frosted glass with a black straw, crushed ice, fresh green mint leaves, and lime wedges.

Both arms are bent and resting on the edge, shoulders and upper body are partially submerged in transparent turquoise water. Her head looks at the camera with a light smile, expression is calm and relaxed. Wet long hair, flawless makeup, lips in a dusty rose shade. Tan skin. She is wearing rimless black sunglasses, a small single gold hoop earring is visible in the ear. Under the water, a black swimsuit on thin straps tied around the neck is visible.

Wet skin.
Square-shaped nude nails.
Almost the entire background is occupied by the saturated blue surface of the pool with lively ripples and light caustics.
```
