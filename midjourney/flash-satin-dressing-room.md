Hair: styled in a high messy updo on the crown, with loose locks falling on the face and neck, creating a soft frame, with a natural matte shine and light texture.

The gaze is directed downwards, the facial expression is focused and sensual. Atmosphere of a dark, luxury walk-in closet.

• LIGHTING: contrasting dramatic lighting with built-in flash, shot on a Canon G7X, creating deep shadows in the background and bright glossy highlights on the satin corset, stockings, and skin.
• TECHNIQUE: Canon G7X, flash, 2000s aesthetic, Kodak Portra 400 film effect, film grain, halation, vignette, analog mood, candid aesthetic, Instagram photo, Pinterest aesthetic, RAW photo. Do not blur the background!
• Camera angle: medium shot (from knees to head), camera at waist level, shot from a slightly low angle to emphasize the length of the legs and the dynamics of pulling the sock.
