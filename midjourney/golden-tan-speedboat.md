Shot on iPhone, vertical lifestyle photography on a luxury yacht. A woman is sitting on the aft sunpad of a moving luxury speedboat. Her head is tilted back towards the sun, chin raised, enjoying the warmth. Sunglasses resting naturally on her face.
Body: feminine hourglass figure with a very thin clearly defined waist, pronounced hips, and a natural full bust enhanced by a supportive push-up fit. Deep golden tropical tan with clearly visible tan lines on shoulders, chest, and hips. Skin is shining with luxurious body oil, creating realistic wet highlights.
Outfit: minimalist knitted light beige triangle bikini with ultra-thin straps and matching bottoms of the same color and material tied high on the waist.
Hair is long below the lower back, blowing in the wind. Glasses are small rimless oval black sunglasses.
Environment: luxury speedboat cruising on sea water. Sunset. Shot with flash. Background is darkened. Do not blur the background. Manicure is short square French.
