## Prompt

```text
A dramatic black and white cinematic portrait of a man with strong, angular features and a well-defined jawline. He gazes intensely into the camera with piercing eyes, his expression brooding and contemplative. Dark, moody studio lighting with a single hard light source positioned high and to the side, creating deep chiaroscuro shadows across half his face. The lighting sculpts his bone structure with precision, emphasizing texture in his skin and the subtle grain characteristic of classic film noir photography. He wears a dark, minimal outfit — a black turtleneck or crew neck that blends into the deep black background. The composition is tight, framing from the chest up with the subject slightly off-center. High contrast processing with rich blacks and bright highlights, no midtone gray — pure dramatic tonal range. Shot with a Sony A7R IV, 85mm f/1.4 GM lens, ISO 100. Rembrandt lighting pattern with no fill, creating a triangle of light on the shadow side of the face. Fine art portrait quality, monochrome, photorealistic, ultra-sharp detail, 8K resolution.
```
