A photo of a man sitting in a restaurant chair. He leans back relaxed, looks at the camera, and holds a glass of red wine in front of him, raising it in a toast. One leg is extended forward, the other is bent, and the pose appears natural and confident. The shot is taken from mid-thigh to head level, angled slightly below eye level. The composition is structured so that the raised glass is in the foreground, while the face remains the main visual focal point.

The man is wearing a loose-fitting white polo shirt (#F5F4F1) made of textured jersey with a vertical rib and a matte finish. His straight-leg trousers are made of thick linen with a matte texture in a warm caramel brown shade (#9C6A45). He wears a chunky metal watch made of polished silver-tone stainless steel (#C5C8CC). A thin silver chain hangs around his neck. He holds a tall, clear glass filled with deep burgundy red wine (#4A0F18).

The scene takes place in an elegant restaurant at night. The man sits in a dark wicker chair at a set table. In the background are other restaurant guests, softly blurred tables, dark walls, and a muted interior devoid of unnecessary detail.

The lighting is based on a flash positioned near the camera. Direct strobe light brightly illuminates his face, clothing, and glass, creating characteristic, sharp highlights on the glass, the metal of the watch, and his skin. The background is dark. The shadows are quite dense but retain detail. The contrast is high, leaving the background significantly darker than the foreground.

Shot with a 35mm lens and flash. Medium depth of field. Focus is on the face. The man's face, hand holding the glass, and the front of his clothing are in maximum focus, while the background is smoothly blurred.
