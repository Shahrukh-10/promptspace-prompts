## Prompt

```text
Photorealistic street portrait of a man, full-body shot from knees to head at the entrance of a dark building: he is standing slightly turned, head lowered, one hand in his pocket, the other hand holding a phone to his ear, as if talking on a call. He is wearing a loose-fitting white linen shirt with long sleeves, dark wide-leg trousers, a dark baseball cap with a small light-colored inscription "Aliev", round dark sunglasses, and a metal watch. The background is nearly black, with a deep interior, muted warm lights, brick paving underfoot, and two decorative black bollards on the edges of the frame. Natural daylight softly illuminates the white shirt, creating strong contrast with the dark background; calm, stylish, slightly cinematic mood. Vertical composition, camera at chest level, slight feel of a candid street shot, realistic textures of fabric, skin, and metal, muted palette of white, black, brown, and warm shadows., 9:16
```
