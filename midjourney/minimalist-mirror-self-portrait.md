## Prompt

```text
I demand perfect and accurate preservation of my facial features: cheekbone lines, eye shape, nose shape, lips, and beard contour. My face must be 100% recognizable and fully correspond to my reference photo. I am captured in a full-body shot, taking a mirror selfie.

My pose: I am standing front-facing in front of a mirror, holding a modern smartphone (iPhone) in a light-colored case at face level, partially covering my nose and mouth, but leaving my eyes and the upper part of my face fully visible. My body is athletic and muscular with broad shoulders and a narrow waist. I am wearing a light (milky white) Polo Ralph Lauren quarter-zip sweater, zipper open to the middle of the chest. On my left wrist, I am wearing a massive silver smartwatch (Apple Watch Ultra) on a wide metal band. I am wearing dark (brown or black) classic trousers with a black leather belt. Glasses in a stylish transparent or metal frame.

Background and environment: I am in a minimalist interior. Behind me is a solid gray wall with a texture of decorative concrete or plaster.

Lighting and style: soft overhead lighting that creates expressive shadows, emphasizing the muscle definition of the arms and shoulders. The photograph has the look of a high-quality mobile mirror shot with natural glares.

Technical execution: extremely high skin detail. I demand a lifelike male texture on the face and hands: pores, hair on the hands, micro-relief of the skin, and tattoo details must be clearly visible.
```
