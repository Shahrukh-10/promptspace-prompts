## Prompt

```text
Use my photo as a reference.
Photorealistic photo 3:4.
MAKEUP: even skin tone with a dewy glow, light highlighter in the inner corners of the eyes, on the cheekbones and bridge of the nose. Long eyeliner wings towards the temples, anime-style cluster false eyelashes (manga lashes). Dusty pink shade on the lips with a soft overlined effect.
APPEARANCE: loose very long straight hair, voluminous blow-dry style. The girl is wearing a corset. Thin silver chain with a small rhinestone heart pendant, matching silver heart-shaped earrings with rhinestones.
POSE: POV-selfie. The girl has her head tilted slightly towards her shoulder, her gaze is directed straight into the camera, she is holding a huge bouquet of white roses that takes up a significant part of the frame. The roses are large, fresh, with soft, slightly open petals. In addition to the roses, the composition includes individual flowers resembling orchids and green elements, adding volume and contrast.
BACKGROUND: modern room interior. Light wall with a marble pattern, the veins are made with a silver glitter paint.
LIGHTING: dimmed lighting, soft, low key. Deep shadows, focus on the light falling on the skin and jewelry, no harsh flash.
COLOR GRADING: muted color palette, matte color correction, effect of a spontaneous photo on the front camera of an iPhone 17 Pro Max.
```
