## Prompt

```text
Use the attached reference photo as the subject.﻿
Keep the face 100% identical to the reference — preserve
every detail, proportions, geometry, expression, skin texture, and all unique
facial features.﻿
Do not modify or stylize the face.﻿
Hair must match the reference photo exactly (same length,
texture, and shape), without mentioning or altering the color.”﻿
 
Create a hyper-realistic mirror selfie scene in a bathroom:﻿
 
The woman stands frontally in front of the mirror, head
slightly tilted to the right, with a soft, subtle smile and a natural
expression.﻿
 
She holds a black iPhone with triple cameras (similar to an
iPhone 14 Pro) in her right hand at face level.﻿
 
Outfit (updated):﻿
 
A silk crop top: smooth, glossy satin/silk texture, thin
straps, natural soft folds in the fabric.﻿
 
Matching silk shorts: short, loose-fitting, made from the
same set, with a delicate satin sheen and natural draping at the waist.﻿
 
A thin necklace with a small pendant remains.﻿
 
The “XIX” Roman numeral tattoo on the left ribcage under the
breast remains.﻿
 
Pose:﻿
 
Standing straight with legs shoulder-width apart.﻿
 
Left arm relaxed and hanging down naturally.﻿
 
Slight arch in the lower back, creating a natural emphasis
on the figure.﻿
 
Eyes looking directly at the camera.﻿
 
Bathroom background:﻿
 
White tiled wall with vertical grout lines.﻿
 
White toilet with closed lid on the right.﻿
 
Sink with chrome faucet and soap dispenser on the left.﻿
 
Metal towel holder on the left; toilet brush on the right.﻿
 
Soft natural daylight, clean highlights on the skin.﻿
 
Minimalistic, clean bathroom atmosphere.﻿
 
Style:﻿
Ultra-realistic, high-resolution, sharp details,
true-to-life textures, accurate mirror reflections.
```
