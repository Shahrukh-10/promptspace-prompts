## Prompt

```text
Extremely detailed, high-fashion beauty portrait. Editorial close-up, luxurious, glamorous, ultra-clean studio atmosphere.

Face and makeup:
Architectural, sharp facial features: pronounced cheekbones, chiseled nose, defined chin oval.
Skin is perfectly even, velvety, premium texture, 'glass skin' effect — smooth and wet glow without any hint of plastic look or heavy retouching.
Glamorous eye makeup with long black eyeliner wings, long thick eyelashes, highlighter on the inner corners and cheekbones.
Eyes are expressive. Eyebrows are wide, dark, clearly groomed upwards in a natural arch, emphasizing the eyes.
Lips are voluminous, perfectly defined, covered in a translucent glossy nude-pink shade, ultra-glossy wet finish with neat light reflections.

Hair and styling:
Perfectly straight hair, styled in a tight, fashionable high ponytail with no gaps and no stray hairs. The entire mass of hair is pulled back smoothly, emphasizing the geometric, clean silhouette of the head.

Accessories, clothing:
None: completely exposed shoulders and décolleté area, no jewelry or distracting details.

Pose and light:
Portrait with head turned slightly to the right and upward (away from the viewer) — chin slightly lifted, gaze directed straight into the camera, facial expression relaxed, confident and sensually neutral, lips slightly parted.
Studio lighting, even, cold-white, emphasizing the wet gloss on the skin, the freshness of the look and the fashionable accent on the texture of the lips and cheekbones.
Clean white background without shadows.

Color grading:
Maximally clean nude and cool tones, high detail of skin and eyes, premium 'beauty editorial' grading, accent fashionable film grain, voluminous rendering of facial texture.

Negative prompts:
No logos, text, clothing, unnecessary accessories, unnatural retouching, reflections and plastic look, no artifacts and other unnecessary elements — only a clean fashionable beauty look.
```
