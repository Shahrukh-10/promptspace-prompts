## Prompt

```text
A photorealistic vertical portrait of a young woman outdoors at twilight, composed like an amateur yet aesthetic smartphone photograph: the model stands in a grassy field, photographed approximately from the waist/hips up at close range, with her body turned sideways and slightly away from the camera, her head turned back over her shoulder, and her gaze directed straight into the lens. She has long, straight, thick hair, worn loose and flowing to the right with the movement, an even part, soft facial features, expressive neat eyebrows, long eyelashes, a calm, slightly thoughtful expression, and slightly parted lips. One hand is raised to the back of her head or hair, with one shoulder exposed; the pose is natural, slightly cinematic and intimate, without excessive posing. Clothing is minimalist: a fitted white bandeau top with exposed shoulders and upper back, featuring black polka dots, a dark bottom consisting of black jeans, and a thin golden chain around her neck. The background is a darkening green field with tall grass, silhouettes of trees and bushes at the sides and in the distance, a low horizon line, and a large sky occupying the upper part of the frame. The sunset sky has a smooth gradient from deep blue at the top to soft pink, lilac, peach, and orange bands near the horizon, with thin clouds stretched horizontally. Lighting is natural twilight lighting: the face and body are softly illuminated by the remaining sky light, the background is darker, with slight underexposure in the shadows and a delicate contrast between the warm sunset and the cool evening blue. The color palette is muted and realistic, with cool green and blue shadows and a warm pink-orange glow at the horizon. The camera is at chest level or slightly below the face, in a vertical 3:4 frame, using a moderately wide-angle smartphone lens, with natural perspective, slight softness and light digital noise in the dark areas, and a slightly blurred but readable background. Style — realistic atmospheric sunset portrait photography, natural skin, soft hair and fabric detail, no studio lighting, no glossy retouching, preserving the feeling of a real moment in nature.
```
