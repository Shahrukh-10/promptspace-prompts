## Prompt

```text
Preserve facial identity, facial features, and hair color exactly as in the reference photo. Shot on Canon G7X Mark III.

Subject: A young woman in front of a stormy, foamy ocean. Hair is thick, long, and natural with soft waves cascading down her back, blowing in the wind, with some strands framing the face. Nails are French manicure, square shape. The woman looks back at the camera with a soft smile. Eyelashes are thick with a wet effect, lips are wearing a nude balm.

Action: She is walking into the ocean while holding a man's hand. Only the man's large hand is visible in the foreground, creating a 'follow me' atmosphere.

Wardrobe: The woman is wearing a white loose oversized t-shirt. Skin is tanned.

Background: Stunning sunset. The sky occupies a large part of the frame and shimmers with bright colors: fiery orange, red, and yellow near the horizon. Clouds are mackerel (cirrocumulus), covering almost the entire sky in a 'scaled' pattern, backlit from below by sunset light.

Technical constraints: Do not blur the background. Exposure is lowered. Shot with flash in the dark. Camera angle is slightly from top to bottom. Aspect ratio 9:16. Strictly correct body proportions, no extra limbs.
```
