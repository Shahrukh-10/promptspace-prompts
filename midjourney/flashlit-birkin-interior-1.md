## Prompt

```text
Preserve facial features and hair color. 100% resemblance. Shot on a Canon G7X Mark III with flash.
A young woman is sitting on a light-colored sofa in a dimly lit interior. The camera is positioned at chest level, with a slight top-down angle. Waist-up framing.
Pose: sitting three-quarters toward the camera. Her right arm rests relaxed on the back of the sofa, while her left arm hangs down in front of her. Her torso is slightly turned toward the lens. Her head is slightly tilted, her gaze directed straight into the camera. Her lips are slightly parted.
Hair: extremely long, extending below the waist, styled with a round brush, cascading haircut, layered shape, ends curled inward, without waves. The hair is swept over to one side.
Clothing: a fitted white tank top with wide straps. Low-rise brown Louis Vuitton monogram jeans.
Accessories: a Rolex watch on her wrist, several thin bracelets, a double chain necklace with a Van Cleef pendant, and a dark brown Hermès Birkin bag with a twilly wrapped around the handle placed beside her on the sofa.
Manicure: short square French manicure.
Background: completely dark, evening atmosphere. The light-colored sofa contrasts with the black background. The interior is almost invisible.
Lighting: hard Canon G7X flash directed straight at the model. The face and body are brightly illuminated while the background remains dark. Slight overexposure on the skin, characteristic flash contrast.
Style: realistic evening lifestyle photograph, the effect of an accidental expensive-looking shot, natural skin texture, high detail, no filters, do not blur the background.
Hair below the waist.
```
