## Prompt

```text
Ultra-realistic intimate bedroom editorial portrait of the same woman, KEEP ORIGINAL FACE 100%, DO NOT ALTER FACIAL IDENTITY.

Shot on an iPhone Pro, vertical 4:5. Almost complete darkness illuminated only by a powerful direct on-camera flash, creating bright glossy highlights, deep black shadows and an authentic late-night candid aesthetic.

The woman lies face down across a large bed covered in charcoal-gray linen bedding. Her head rests sideways on her folded arms while she looks softly toward the camera with a sleepy, relaxed expression. Her long damp hair falls naturally across her back and shoulders with a wet, slightly tousled texture.

Her bare back is visible while the lower body is discreetly covered by dark charcoal linen bedding and oversized pillows. The composition remains elegant and understated.

Minimalist bedroom with a smooth dark gray wall, oversized charcoal linen pillows, crumpled stone-washed linen bedding and almost no visible ambient light beyond the flash.

Authentic iPhone direct flash color grading: intentionally underexposed scene, deep black shadows, muted charcoal-gray tones, warm natural skin, glossy flash highlights, crisp flash shadows, realistic flash falloff, subtle digital grain, realistic smartphone HDR, high micro-contrast, authentic luxury night flash photography, photorealistic textures, quiet luxury aesthetic, 8K.
```
