## Prompt

```text
A man sits astride a white horse, his body turned three-quarters, his shoulders facing the camera. One hand holds the reins, the other rests on the horse's back. His head is slightly turned to the side, his gaze directed toward the camera. The frame is full, with both the man and the horse shown almost entirely, and the composition is horizontally balanced.

The scene is shot in black and white, with a pronounced contrast between the horse's light coat, light-colored clothing, and the darker background.

The man's clothing is kept in a light, minimalist palette. He wears a light, soft shirt and a light sweater draped over his shoulders, tied in front. He wears loose-fitting, light-colored trousers, the fabric matte, with soft folds. The look is elegant and relaxed, reminiscent of a classic fashion shoot.

The horse is large, white, with smooth coat and a neat bridle. The head is turned slightly toward the camera, ears raised, and the pose is calm and confident.

The location is an open grassy field. Trees and a mountainous landscape with soft rolling hills are visible in the background. The background is slightly blurred, creating a sense of depth.

The lighting is natural sunlight, from the side and slightly above. The light emphasizes the volume of the horse's body and the texture of its clothing. The contrast is medium to high, typical of black-and-white fine art photography.

Shot with an 85mm lens, moderate depth of field, focusing on the man and the horse's head, and a softly blurred background. High detail in the fur, fabric, and grass, fine art black and white portrait photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.
```
