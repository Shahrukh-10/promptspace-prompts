## Prompt

```text
Do not change facial features and hair color. Preserve 100% identity. This selfie depicts a girl with natural cheekbone contouring. 

Brows: thick, perfectly groomed with a lamination effect, precise shape. 
Eyes: Makeup in warm brown tones with soft-blended eyeshadow, classic black graphic cat-eye eyeliner, long eyelashes. 
Lips: Clearly defined contour with dark liner, matte lipstick in a "dusty rose" or warm brown nude shade. 

Hair: silky and thick. Falling naturally, partially covering the side of the face like a veil. 
Facial expression: alluring. 
Clothing: tight-fitting sleeveless milky-white mini dress made of thick knit fabric, the fabric tightly follows the line of the chest, waist, and hips, creating natural folds, tension, and deep shadows. 
Background: minimalist interior with an empty warm gray-beige wall without details, background is separated. 
Lighting: photo in the dark with deep shadows. 
Pose: body slightly turned to the side, one hand is at the left hip, fingers are relaxed and slightly bent, the palm gracefully lifts the edge of the dress, causing the lower part of the fabric to softly spread and reveal the side line of the hip. Torso is tilted. 
Camera angle: low-angle selfie, close-up. 
Style: focus on silhouette, hand, and sculptural texture of the clothing. 
Atmosphere: intimate, tightly cropped composition. 
Milky long square nails.

Photorealism, high detail of skin, fabric, and props. 
Image quality equivalent to iPhone 17. 

Natural skin tones with slightly desaturated shadows and boosted contrast. Realistic matte-velvety skin texture, visible pores, soft highlight on raised areas. High detail on face and clothing. Strictly preserve original facial features and appearance. Aspect ratio 9:16
```
