## Prompt

```text
A beautiful girl with slicked-back wet hair gathered in a low bun, standing chest-deep in crystal clear turquoise tropical ocean water. The right hand is raised to her forehead to shield eyes from the sun, fingers touching the forehead; the left hand is lowered along the body. The body is turned three-quarters to the left, head in profile looking to the left. Perfect bronze tanned skin, wet and glistening from the water with visible droplets. She is wearing a bright lemon-yellow bandeau swimsuit, strapless, with large circular cutouts on the sides at the waist exposing skin. White square-shaped manicure on nails, gold ribbed fan-shaped seashell earrings in ears. To her right, a medium-sized sea turtle with a brown-gray spotted shell swims, showing details of the shell, head, and front flippers partially submerged. The water is absolutely transparent turquoise-blue with light waves, caustic light patterns, and sun glints on the surface with light ripples. White sandy bottom is clearly visible through the water. Bright sunny midday light creates contrasting shadows on the skin, especially from the hand on the face.
```
