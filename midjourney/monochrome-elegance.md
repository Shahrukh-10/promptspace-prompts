## Prompt

```text
A striking high-contrast black-and-white portrait of a young man captured in a classic monochrome studio style. He has a strong jawline and well-groomed short dark hair, wearing a crisp white dress shirt with the collar slightly open, projecting quiet confidence and understated sophistication. The lighting setup features a single key light positioned at 45 degrees camera-right with a large softbox creating sculpted Rembrandt-style shadows across his face, complemented by a subtle hair light from behind to separate him from the deep black background. Shot on a medium format digital camera with an 80mm portrait lens at f/2.8, delivering exceptional skin detail and tonal gradation from pure whites to rich, inky blacks. The monochrome conversion emphasizes texture in the skin and fabric while maintaining smooth tonal transitions. Grain is minimal and controlled, reminiscent of Ilford Delta 100 film. The subject gazes directly into the lens with a composed, thoughtful expression. Composition is a tight head-and-shoulders crop centered in frame, evoking timeless editorial portraiture with an air of refined masculine elegance.
```
