## Prompt

```text
Realistic low-light smartphone snapshot of an adult man sitting casually in a dark outdoor restaurant or upscale lounge at night. Camera positioned across the table at seated eye level, framing him approximately from knees to head. He sits deep in a cushioned chair or sofa with his legs relaxed and spread naturally beneath the table. He wears a fitted plain black short-sleeve T-shirt, loose cream or off-white trousers, narrow dark sunglasses, a watch and several subtle bracelets. One hand lifts a stemmed glass of red wine directly to his mouth as he takes a sip. His other hand rests forward near the table holding a lit cigar between his fingers. A large black rectangular ashtray sits directly in front of him with small cigar remnants inside. A dark phone, small personal items and a patterned pouch or wallet sit casually on the pale stone tabletop. The background is extremely dark with scattered restaurant chairs, tables, tiny warm lamps and a few illuminated green tree branches. Most of the surroundings disappear naturally into shadow. The scene should feel like a random late-night phone picture, not a staged luxury campaign. Shot on an iPhone 17 Pro Max rear camera in very low available light. Automatic phone exposure brightens the man's face and clothing slightly while leaving the background almost black. Visible digital noise, slightly crushed shadows, mild loss of detail, imperfect focus, subtle motion softness around the raised wine glass, realistic phone-camera processing. NO professional restaurant photography, NO cinematic lighting, NO flash photography, NO artificial rim light, NO luxury advertisement, NO dramatic smoke effects, NO fake bokeh, NO HDR glow, NO editorial composition, NO skin retouching. Keep the darkness, grain, imperfect exposure and ordinary smartphone quality.
```
