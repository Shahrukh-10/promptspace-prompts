## Prompt

```text
Create a portrait of the person in the uploaded reference image. Preserve identity 100% — preserve facial features, skin tone, eye color, hair color, hair texture, and recognizable body proportions exactly. Do not alter the face.

Place the person standing in the middle of a lush outdoor flower garden filled densely with blooming white roses and other small white blossoms, with green foliage extending throughout the background. The scene is photographed during golden hour, with the low sun positioned behind the subject toward the left side of the frame, creating a bright glowing horizon, warm rim light, and soft atmospheric flare through the flowers. Compose the image vertically as a three-quarter to nearly full-body fashion portrait, photographed from approximately eye level with a slightly intimate perspective. The person stands naturally among the flowers, body angled subtly to one side while facing toward the camera, with one arm relaxed beside the body and the other bent gently at the waist. Keep the posture poised and confident, with a soft, relaxed expression and direct engagement with the camera. Dress the person in a fitted blush-pink long-sleeve mini dress with a feminine silhouette, a rounded scoop neckline, close-fitting sleeves that widen subtly toward the wrists, and gathered ruched detailing around the hips and lower portion of the dress. The fabric should appear soft and slightly stretchy with realistic folds and gentle highlights. Keep accessories minimal and understated so the floral setting remains dominant. Style the hair long, smooth, and straight, falling naturally over the shoulders and down the front, with a clean polished arrangement while retaining the user's original hair color exactly. Use warm backlighting from the setting sun combined with soft ambient daylight illuminating the front of the subject, producing glowing hair edges, delicate highlights on the pink fabric, and natural shadows. The palette is dominated by blush pink, creamy white, rich botanical green, and warm golden sunlight. Create a romantic, dreamy, feminine garden atmosphere. Preserve individual flower petals, leaves, natural fabric texture, subtle skin detail, realistic hair strands, and authentic sunlight diffusion. Use an elegant editorial fashion-photography aesthetic with shallow depth of field, softly blurred background flowers, natural lens flare, warm color grading, gentle contrast, and realistic photographic rendering.

Keep the pose, wardrobe, setting, camera angle, and lighting exactly as described. Preserve natural anatomy, believable skin texture, and photographic realism. The result must look like a real photograph of the reference person taken in this scene — not a paste-in composite. Match the color grading and mood consistently across the whole frame.
```
