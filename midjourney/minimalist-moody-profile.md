## Prompt

```text
Minimalist photorealistic street portrait in vertical frame: young adult man positioned in the lower right part of the image, shot close-up from shoulders up in a strict side profile, looking to the left, gaze calm and slightly lowered, expression reserved and thoughtful. He has a short dark beard, a sharp profile of nose and lips, head covered by a simple black tight-fitting beanie pulled low over forehead and ears. Clothing is completely dark: black hoodie or jacket with hood, hood resting on the back of the neck, matte fabric with soft folds, shoulder silhouette extending to the right edge of the frame. Shot from approximately eye level, portrait perspective without distortion, sharp focus on the face, beard, beanie, and shoulder line, background heavily blurred. Most of the frame is occupied by empty cold gray-blue sky; at the bottom, a blurred line of trees and a misty mountain landscape are barely visible. Light is soft and diffused, overcast day, no harsh shadows or highlights, palette is muted: black, graphite, smoky gray, and pale bluish-gray. Mood is quiet, cold, cinematic, in the style of restrained documentary fashion/editorial photography. 9:16
```
