## Prompt

```text
Create a hyper-realistic image featuring a stylish person with the same face as the original reference (accurate facial preservation and close reference matching).
The person is wearing:
A light grey knitted sweater (soft, minimal Tom-themed tone)
High-waisted denim blue jeans
White high-top sneakers
Pose & Interaction: The person is standing confidently, with one arm wrapped around a giant, 3D photorealistic Tom.
Jerry is sitting playfully on Tom’s shoulder.
Expressions & Mood:
The person has a fun, mischievous expression
Tom appears confident and bold
Jerry looks playful and cheeky
Characters:
Tom & Jerry, rendered as a 3D photorealistic duo, seamlessly integrated with the human subject
Environment:
A clean grey-blue backdrop, minimal and studio-like, keeping focus on the subjects
Overall Style:
Ultra-detailed
Cinematic lighting
Hyper-realistic textures
High-resolution, polished finish
```
