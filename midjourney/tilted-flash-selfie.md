## Prompt

```text
Do not change the girl's appearance from the photo. Selfie portrait in low-light conditions. The girl has very long, loose, straight hair, slightly carelessly swept to one side.
An earring—a thin chain—and a white earbud are visible in her ear. A silver watch is visible on her wrist. Manicure: sharp, long, milky-colored nails.
She is wearing a tight-fitting nude-colored long-sleeve top made of mesh fabric with a tattoo-like print covering the entire garment, through which a black lace bra is slightly visible.
Camera angle from an outstretched arm at eye level, with a strongly tilted horizon, close-up. The hand covers the lower part of the face, including the chin; playful expression, lips slightly closed. The hand movement is slightly blurred.
Background: a very dark, blurred, indistinct light-colored modern interior. Dramatic low-key lighting with very strong directional light, creating a flash effect, extreme highlights, and high contrast, emphasizing the texture of the skin and the shine of the highlighter. High skin detail, visible pores.
Photo taken with an iPhone 17 Pro camera, live candid-shot effect. 2:3
```
