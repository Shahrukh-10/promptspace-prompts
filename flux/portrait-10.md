## Prompt

```text
A hyper-realistic portrait of the same woman from the reference photo, keeping her facial features 100% unchanged.  Outfit She wears a form-fitting little black dress with a unique halter-style neckline, where the straps wrap around the neck like a soft scarf or long ribbons that fall elegantly down the back. Sheer pantyhose add a chic, polished touch. On her feet are glossy patent pointed-toe heels: the left shoe is worn normally on the floor, while the right shoe hangs loosely from her toes as her raised foot rests on the chair, creating a relaxed, slightly tired aesthetic.  Makeup Douyin/Igari-style makeup with a flushed, emotional finish. Pink–coral blush is applied generously across the cheeks and the tip of the nose for a naturally heated, soft-drunk effect. Sharp eyeliner defines the eyes, with lightly emphasized aegyosal and curled lashes. Her lips have a glossy rosy/brick-toned finish, appearing full and dewy. Skin looks smooth, glowing, and hydrated.  Hairstyle Her hair is styled in a messy bun or spiky updo with loose face-framing strands for an effortless, Y2K–office siren mood. (No color specified.)  Pose & Expression The woman sits on a minimalist metal chair with a black cushion. Her pose is asymmetrical: left foot on the floor, right leg bent up onto the chair. Her right hand supports her cheek with the elbow resting on her raised knee, giving a dreamy, bored, subtly seductive mood. Her left hand holds a vintage telephone receiver, the cord hanging freely. She looks directly into the camera with an intense, flat yet captivating expression from behind glasses worn slightly low on the nose.  Accessories Thin silver wire-rimmed glasses worn low on the nose. A simple silver ring on the left ring finger. The dress’s neck ribbon functions like a soft black choker.  Background & Props A clean studio setup with a gradient grey seamless backdrop. On the floor: a vintage beige rotary phone (base on the floor, receiver in her hand) and scattered fashion magazines. To the left, a wooden table edge is visible with a paper coffee cup and a desk calendar showing the number “5”. On the right side of the floor lies a rigid black briefcase or box.  Lighting Soft directional studio lighting that creates subtle depth without harsh shadows, enhancing the glow of the skin, lips, and glossy shoes while sculpting the facial features.
```
