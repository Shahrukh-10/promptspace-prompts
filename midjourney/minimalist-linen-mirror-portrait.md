## Prompt

```text
A man takes a mirror selfie in a modern, minimalist interior. He stands directly in front of a large round mirror, holding a smartphone with one hand and the other relaxed in his pocket. His gaze is focused on the phone screen. The shot is taken from the hips to the head, shot at eye level through the reflection in the mirror.

The man is wearing a loose, oversized, fine linen shirt with a charcoal gray (RGB 74, 74, 74) check pattern with a matte texture, unbuttoned at the top buttons and rolled up at the sleeves. He is wearing wide-leg trousers in ivory white (RGB 244, 240, 232) made of dense cotton twill with a matte finish. He is holding an iPhone.

The shoot takes place in a bright, modern room with a minimalist interior. A large, round, frameless mirror is positioned in front of the model. An abstract painting in a thin wooden frame hangs on the wall, with a floor tripod partially visible to the right. The walls are a solid warm light beige, and the interior is decorated in a calm, neutral palette.

The lighting is soft and natural. The contrast is moderately low, the light is even, and there are no harsh glare.

Shot on an iPhone. High detail is captured on the skin, the fabric of the linen shirt, the texture of the hair, the reflection of the mirror, and interior elements. Lifestyle mirror photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.
```
